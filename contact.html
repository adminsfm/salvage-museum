Donations and Loans

Please contact the museum before bringing artifacts, documents, or photographs for review by the collections committee. Note that we have limited capacity for new acquisitions at this time. 

Before You Visit

The museum is open seasonally and admission is by donation. Visitors are encouraged to sign the guest book, ask questions, browse the gift shop, and continue their journey in Salvage through the numerous hiking trails on the Damnable Trail system.
